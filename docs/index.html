How to play
  1. Install: up to parts per turn from the 3 offered. Pick a part, then click a gear slot (occupied slots are replaced). Parts you don't install are discarded.
  2. Pick a direction for the whole turn. The gear turns one step for free and the part at the top (▼) triggers.
  3. Crank more steps in the same direction for 1 Crank Power each ( per turn).
  4. Every trigger adds 1 Heat (some parts add more). At Heat the machine overheats: the turn stops, Heat resets to 0, and next turn nothing can crank. Heat past the limit is forgiven.
  5. Block resets every turn. The enemy acts at the end of your turn — its intent is shown. Win by bringing its HP to 0.
